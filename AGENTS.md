# AI Agent Guidelines

These guidelines are shared by Copilot, Antigravity, and any other agent used in this repository. The specifications in `docs/specs/` are the source of truth for the product.

## Before starting work

- Read these guidelines and the `docs/specs/` documents relevant to the task.
- Treat confirmed specifications and accepted decisions as constraints. Treat `To be defined` fields and open decisions as unknown; do not invent requirements, product domain, undecided technologies, or behavior.
- Follow the confirmed architectural principles: modular monolith, simplicity, low operational cost, maintainability, responsiveness, and future evolvability. Do not introduce microservices, Kubernetes, messaging infrastructure, distributed cache, or other unnecessary components without an explicit confirmed requirement and requester approval.
- Follow the confirmed responsive UI specification: Bootstrap 5, with a header, collapsible navigation menu, content area, and footer, supporting desktop, tablet, and smartphone viewports. Do not invent exact breakpoints or menu interactions beyond the specs.
- Follow the confirmed visual color system in `docs/specs/03-ux-and-accessibility.md`, including token values, intended use, light/dark theme roles, and the approximate 60/30/10 color balance. Treat reference images as chromatic references only; do not copy their layout, components, or structure. Validate contrast, and do not assume an accessibility conformance level not specified there.
- Follow the confirmed Docker local development requirement: provide a root `Dockerfile` for the ASP.NET Core application and a root `docker-compose.yml` for running it with PostgreSQL locally. This does not imply production containerization; do not invent unspecified Docker settings as confirmed requirements.
- The confirmed initial product scope includes patients and responsible persons, optional dated anamnesis records with preserved revisions, scheduled and realized patient attendance/evolution records, and professional registration initially limited to fonoaudiólogos: unique CPF, certifications (description, alphanumeric registration, completion date), telephone/email contacts, and create/modify/inactivate/delete flows. During patient registration, association with zero or more existing, active professionals is optional; an active professional may also be added later through the patient record, and a professional may be associated with multiple patients. Scheduling requires one or more active professionals associated with the patient by the time scheduling completes; if none are actively associated, the scheduling flow offers all active professionals and creates associations for the selection. Any system user with scheduling permission may schedule or reschedule; which roles have this permission remains open. A professional associated with a patient cannot be deleted. Professional council information via public API is deferred. Attendance status flow, scheduling, realization times, cancellation, rescheduling, and evolution ownership are specified in `docs/specs/05-use-cases.md`; do not contradict them. Anamnesis revision metadata and attendance/evolution access controls and privacy remain open; attendance/evolution records are retained permanently and their specified audit metadata is complete. Consult `docs/specs/05-use-cases.md`; do not infer open fields, validations, responsible-person/contact cardinality, contact/certification requiredness or multiplicity, CPF validation, professional deletion semantics, remaining cancellation-reason values and maintenance permissions, or other features.
- The initial scope includes scheduling and maintaining patient attendances. Each attendance has a required scheduled date/time, a duration selected from the fixed options of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, or 60 minutes, and one or more active professionals associated with the patient by the time scheduling completes; when no active association exists, the scheduling flow creates it from the selected active-professional registry entry. A replacement created by rescheduling requires a new duration selection. Any participating professional may start, end, or cancel an attendance while allowed by its status, and actual start/end times are recorded. Inactivation does not alter existing attendance participation or prevent otherwise-allowed evolution updates. Each participating professional has one optional current evolution entry with a required therapeutic-plan field and optional observations; updates replace the current content without preserving prior versions, and professionals may record or update only their own entry while the attendance is `Em andamento` or `Aguardando Evolução`. One evolution is sufficient for status `Finalizado`, while participants without an evolution remain individually indicated as pending. Attendance and evolution records are retained permanently and cannot be deleted. The specified states, cancellation and rescheduling flows, late indication, and participant maintenance limits are in `docs/specs/05-use-cases.md`. No other attendance data may be edited. Attendance/evolution access controls for other roles, privacy, and remaining cancellation-reason values and maintenance permissions remain open; the specified audit metadata is complete.
- Use `docs/specs/06-implementation-checklist.md` as the master implementation roadmap. When asked what remains, report pending, blocked, and in-progress items in its hierarchy, exclude done items, and explain blockers. Update item status during implementation; mark an item done only after its implementation and relevant validation pass. Add approved new work to the roadmap before implementing it, without inventing scope or changing completed history.
- If a decision could change scope, user experience, data, security, or cost, ask for confirmation before marking it as decided.
- During this setup phase, work only on agent artifacts and specifications. Do not create application code.

## Maintaining specifications

- Write in concise, verifiable English.
- Distinguish confirmed requirements, assumptions, and open questions.
- Give requirements and acceptance criteria stable identifiers.
- Record significant decisions in `docs/specs/decisions/`, including context, decision, and consequences.
- Update the index in `docs/specs/README.md` when adding or renaming documents.

## Using multiple tools

- Copilot and Antigravity should consult these guidelines and the specifications before proposing or performing work.
- When using Grok through its website/app, provide `AGENTS.md` and only the relevant specification documents as context. State the goal explicitly and ask it to preserve open questions rather than assume answers.
- Do not include credentials, tokens, or personal data in prompts or context documents.
