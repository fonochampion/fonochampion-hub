# Data and Integrations

**Status:** Draft
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-11

## Data

- **Required entities and relationships:** Patient (full name, date of birth, derived age, CID) with zero or more responsible-person records; each responsible-person record belongs to one patient and has full name, a degree of kinship, and one or more telephone/email contacts. Patient creation rejects a duplicate normalized full name and date of birth as defined in [ADR-028](./decisions/028-patient-duplicate-detection.md). Degree-of-kinship values remain to be defined. Anamnesis records are optional, dated, and preserve revisions with each version's date, author, and timestamp. Each user account has exactly one initial role: Administrator, Attendant, or Therapist. Role permissions are defined in [05 — Use Cases](./05-use-cases.md). Each attendance concerns one patient and one or more professionals who are active and associated with the patient when scheduling completes. Every attendance has a required scheduled date/time and automatically recorded actual start/end date/time; its statuses and rescheduling relationship are defined in UC-008. Each participating professional has one optional current evolution entry with separate therapeutic-plan and observation fields; the plan is required, observations are optional, updates replace the current content without preserving prior versions, and only the participating professional may record or update their own entry while the attendance is `Em andamento` or `Aguardando Evolução`. An Administrator may view all patient clinical data but cannot alter another professional's evolution. If no active professional is associated when scheduling, the flow offers the active professional registry and creates patient-professional associations for selected professionals. Active professionals may also be added to a patient through the patient record after initial registration; associations may be removed without deleting or altering existing attendance/evolution records, and already scheduled attendances and their participants remain unchanged. Patient records may optionally be associated during registration with one or more existing, active professionals. A professional may be associated with multiple patients. Professional records are initially limited to fonoaudiólogos and require a full name and CPF. CPF accepts 11 digits or the standard `000.000.000-00` format, passes check-digit validation, and is stored and compared using only its 11 digits. Certifications are optional and multiple certifications are allowed; each has a description, alphanumeric registration, and completion date, which cannot be in the future. At least one telephone or email contact is required, multiple values of either type are allowed, and contacts follow the Brazilian telephone and standard email validation rules in [ADR-031](./decisions/031-professional-record-rules.md). See [05 — Use Cases](./05-use-cases.md).
- **Attendance status history:** The system preserves each status change with its author and date/time. For an automatic transition, the author is the professional or user whose action triggered it.
- **Appointment duration:** Each attendance has a duration selected from the fixed options of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, or 60 minutes. The duration is used with the scheduled date/time to prevent overlaps. A replacement created by rescheduling requires a new duration selection.
- **Professional CPF:** CPF is required, must pass check-digit validation, and defines professional uniqueness. Input accepts digits or the standard punctuation; storage and comparisons use only its 11 digits.
- **Data sources and ownership:** The requester identifies the supplied Brazilian Portuguese CID-10 files as SUS data; their upstream edition/version and terms have not been independently verified. Use is restricted to internal environments; do not commit, package, or redistribute the source files or imported catalog data. Each environment supplies the files locally for import (see [ADR-026](./decisions/026-local-cid10-reference-catalog.md)). Sources and ownership for other data remain to be defined.
- **Personal or sensitive data:** Patient and responsible-person records include names, date of birth, derived age, CID, kinship, telephone, and email. Anamnesis and attendance evolution contain patient health and clinical information. Professional records include full name, CPF, certification details, and contact information. System-user accounts include username, display name, email attribute, credential, role, and active state. Email is not used for authentication. Temporary passwords for accounts created by an Administrator are shown once and must be changed at first login; the initial Administrator is provisioned from external configuration and must change that password at first login. Password reset/recovery is deferred from the first implementation delivery. Clinical-feature implementation is blocked pending privacy/legal review of consent/legal basis, handling requirements, and retention.
- **Retention, deletion, and export:** Attendance records and evolution entries are retained permanently and cannot be deleted. Anamneses are not automatically deleted while a retention policy is pending; their final retention and deletion rules remain to be defined. Other data-retention rules remain open.

## Assumptions and open questions

- Define the closed list of kinship values and responsible-person name-normalization rules.
- Define patient record update and archival behavior.
- Role permissions and the initial username/password, session, failed-login, first-login password-change, Administrator bootstrap, and account-field policies are defined in UC-009 and ADR-022/ADR-023/ADR-024. The first implementation delivery uses three fixed roles, an externally configured initial Administrator, and one-time temporary passwords with forced change at first login. Define access-reset mechanics, consent/legal basis, data-protection requirements, and retention.
- Appointment scheduling is included in the current scope. Administrators and Attendants may schedule or reschedule. If the patient has no active associated professional, the scheduling flow offers all active professionals and creates associations for the selection. Each attendance has a duration selected from the fixed options of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, or 60 minutes and used with its scheduled date/time to prevent overlaps. A professional's existing `Agendado` attendances prevent overlap; other attendance statuses do not count as scheduling conflicts. Overlapping attendances for the same patient are prevented.
- Define which professional fields may be edited and how certifications and contacts are individually maintained.
- Professional associations may be removed without deleting or altering existing attendance/evolution records; already scheduled attendances and their participants remain unchanged. Professional inactivation retains existing associations, prevents new scheduling involving the inactive professional, and reactivation restores the associations. Inactivation does not alter existing attendance participation or prevent otherwise-allowed evolution updates. Rescheduling removes inactive participants; if none remain, the flow selects active associated professionals or, if none are associated, creates associations from the active professional registry.
- Anamnesis access is available to Administrators and Therapists for all patients. Anamnesis sections use free text and each version records its date, author, and timestamp. Automatic deletion is not allowed pending a retention policy. The defined role access does not unblock clinical implementation: privacy/legal review must define consent/legal basis, handling requirements, and retention.
- Attendance and evolution records are retained permanently and cannot be deleted. Administrators and Therapists may access clinical attendance/evolution data for all patients; only participating professionals may update their own evolution. Administrators may cancel any `Agendado` attendance; Therapists may cancel only as participants, and Attendants cannot cancel. Clinical-feature implementation remains blocked pending privacy/legal review.
- Administrators maintain configurable cancellation reasons `Não comparecimento`, `Indisponibilidade do profissional`, and `Solicitação do paciente/responsável`. Configurable reasons have a required unique name (unique across active and inactive records), an optional description, and an active/inactive indicator; they support create, list, edit, and inactivate operations and cannot be deleted. An inactivated reason is unavailable for new cancellations but remains on historical attendances, and editing a reason does not alter the original name and description shown in historical attendances. `Remarcação` is a fixed system reason assigned automatically during rescheduling.
- Administrators create/edit user accounts, assign or change exactly one of the three fixed roles, and activate/inactivate accounts. Local authentication uses username and password; email is not the login identifier. Newly created accounts receive a system-generated temporary password displayed once and must change it at first login. The initial Administrator is provisioned from external configuration and must change the configured password at first login. Reset/recovery and account deletion are outside the first implementation delivery; reset mechanics and deletion remain to be defined.
- Select and assess a public professional-council API before implementing that later-stage integration; no provider has been selected.
