# Requirements

**Status:** Draft
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-30

## Functional requirements

Record each confirmed requirement with a stable identifier and observable acceptance criteria.

| ID | Requisito | Critérios de aceite | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Register a patient with full name, age, and associations to professionals who will attend the patient. | An Administrator or Attendant can create a patient record with full name and age and may associate zero or more already registered, active professionals during registration. An active, registered professional may also be added later through the patient record. If no active professional is associated when scheduling an attendance, the scheduling flow offers the active registry and creates patient-professional associations for the selected professionals. Each professional may be associated with multiple patients. An association may be removed without deleting or altering existing attendance/evolution records; already scheduled attendances and their participants remain unchanged. Age representation and validation remain to be defined. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-002 | Associate a list of responsible persons with a patient. | An Administrator or Attendant can associate responsible persons with a patient; each responsible person has a full name and a degree of kinship. The kinship domain, cardinality constraints, and validation remain to be defined. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-003 | Register telephone and email contact details for a responsible person. | An Administrator or Attendant can register telephone and email contact details for a responsible person. Requiredness, multiplicity, and validation remain to be defined. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-004 | Record the patient's CID (International Classification of Diseases). | CID is a patient attribute. Retrieval from a public API is planned for a later stage; the API, integration behavior, and whether CID is available before that stage remain to be defined. | Confirmed; integration deferred |
| FR-005 | Register professionals, initially limited to fonoaudiólogos. | An Administrator can create a professional record with basic data, certifications, and contacts. The specific basic-data fields and the meaning of extensibility to other professions remain open. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-006 | Record professional certifications. | Each certification contains a description, an alphanumeric registration, and a completion date. Whether certifications are required or multiple certifications are allowed remains open. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-007 | Record professional contacts. | Professional contact types include telephone and email. Requiredness, multiplicity, and validation remain open. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-008 | Modify a professional record. | An Administrator can alter an existing professional record. Editable fields and validation remain open; a CPF already belonging to another professional must not be accepted. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-009 | Inactivate a professional. | An Administrator can inactivate a professional record. Existing patient associations are retained; the professional cannot be used for new scheduling while inactive. Reactivation restores the retained associations automatically. Inactivation does not alter existing attendance participation; the professional may continue participating and record/update their own evolution under the normal attendance-status rules. | Confirmed |
| FR-010 | Delete a professional only when no patient is associated with that professional. | The system prevents deletion while at least one patient is associated with the professional. An Administrator may delete a professional only when no patient is associated. A professional may be associated with multiple patients. Whether deletion is physical or logical remains open. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-011 | Retrieve professional council-registration information from a public API. | This integration is planned for a later stage and is not part of the current implementation scope. API provider, data, and behavior remain open. | Confirmed; deferred |
| FR-012 | Record and maintain a patient's dated anamnesis history. | Patient registration may omit anamnesis. When provided, an anamnesis has a required performance date and the confirmed sections listed in [UC-001](./05-use-cases.md#uc-001--register-a-patient-and-record-anamnesis); all other fields are optional and may combine structured values with free-text observations. Each new anamnesis is a separate record; editing an existing record preserves prior versions and change history. Administrators and Therapists can access anamneses for all patients; detailed prompts, version metadata, and privacy/handling requirements remain open. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-013 | Schedule and maintain patient attendances and per-professional evolution entries. | Each attendance is scheduled with a date/time, a duration selected from fixed options of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, or 60 minutes, and one or more active professionals associated with the patient. Administrators and Attendants may schedule or reschedule; the flow offers associated active professionals or, if none exist, all active professionals and creates associations for the selection. A participating Therapist may start or end their attendance and may cancel it while `Agendado`; an Administrator may cancel any `Agendado` attendance, while an Attendant may not cancel. Attendants and Administrators may add or remove participants under the conditions in [UC-008](./05-use-cases.md#uc-008--record-and-maintain-a-patient-attendance). Administrators and Therapists may access attendance and evolution data for all patients; Attendants may access operational scheduling data but not anamnesis or evolution content. Each participating professional may record or replace only their own optional evolution entry in allowed statuses; prior versions are not retained, and missing entries remain individually indicated. Rescheduling cancels the existing scheduled attendance and creates a replacement for the same patient; it keeps existing active participants, removes inactive participants, and selects replacement professionals if no active participant remains. The replacement is linked to the prior record, assigned the fixed `Remarcação` reason automatically, and given a new duration selection. Only an Administrator maintains configurable cancellation reasons; additional values and clinical-data privacy/handling requirements remain open. Attendance records and evolution entries cannot be deleted. No other attendance data may be edited. | Confirmed; details open |
| FR-014 | Manage system-user accounts and assign initial access roles. | An Administrator can create and edit user accounts; assign or change exactly one role per account (`Administrador`, `Atendente`, or `Terapeuta`); activate or inactivate an account; and initiate access reset. Account deletion is not specified. Account fields, authentication, reset mechanics, and initial Administrator provisioning remain open. | Confirmed; details open |

The initial functional scope includes patient/responsible-person/contact registration, dated anamnesis records, scheduled and realized attendance records, professional registration/maintenance, and system-user account management described in [05 — Use Cases](./05-use-cases.md). The professional-council API is deferred. Do not infer billing or other features from the product description.

## Non-functional requirements

Include only necessary, confirmed requirements, such as performance, availability, security, privacy, compatibility, and accessibility. Use verifiable metrics where possible.

| ID | Category | Requirement and measure | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-001 | Architecture | The application must use a modular monolith. Acceptance: deliver the application as a single deployable system with modules, not as independently deployed microservices. | Confirmed |
| NFR-002 | Operational cost | Prefer low operational cost and avoid unnecessary infrastructure. Acceptance: each additional operational component is justified by an explicit, confirmed requirement; do not add components for hypothetical scale. | Confirmed |
| NFR-003 | Maintainability and evolution | Favor simple, maintainable solutions that support incremental evolution. Acceptance: proposed changes use the confirmed project structure and introduce no architectural component or abstraction without a demonstrated requirement. | Confirmed |
| NFR-004 | Responsiveness | The user interface must support desktop, tablet, and smartphone viewports using the confirmed standard layout. Acceptance: validate defined primary screens in all three device classes, with usable navigation and no loss of the header, content area, or footer. | Confirmed |
| NFR-005 | Local development | Provide a Docker-based local environment for the ASP.NET Core application and PostgreSQL. Acceptance: the repository includes `Dockerfile` and `docker-compose.yml` configured to run both services locally. | Confirmed |

## Assumptions and open questions

- Age representation, CID availability before the future API integration, kinship domain values, validation, duplicate handling, and create/read/update/delete lifecycle for these records remain open.
- Contact requiredness and multiplicity remain open.
- Professional basic-data fields, CPF requiredness/validation, certification requiredness/multiplicity, and contact requiredness/multiplicity remain open.
- Professional associations are optional during patient registration; active, registered professionals may be added later through the patient record or, when none are actively associated, selected and associated as part of scheduling. Each attendance must have one or more active associated professionals when scheduling completes. An association may be removed without deleting or altering existing attendance/evolution records; already scheduled attendances and their participants remain unchanged. Professional inactivation retains patient associations, prevents new scheduling involving the inactive professional, and reactivation restores the associations. Inactivation does not alter existing attendance participation or prevent otherwise-allowed evolution updates.
- Anamnesis records are optional during patient registration, have a required performance date, and preserve previous records and revisions. Administrators and Therapists can access all patients' anamneses. Detailed prompts, revision metadata, privacy, and retention remain open.
- Attendance records concern a patient and one or more associated active professionals and always include a scheduled date/time and a duration selected from fixed options of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, or 60 minutes; attendance records and evolution entries cannot be deleted. Administrators and Therapists can access all patients' attendance/evolution data; Attendants cannot access anamnesis/evolution content. Each participating professional has one optional current evolution entry and may maintain only their own entry; Administrators cannot override this authorship rule. See UC-008 for status transitions, cancellation, rescheduling, late indication, and actual realization timestamps. Administrators maintain configurable cancellation reasons; remaining values and clinical-data privacy/handling requirements remain open.
- The three initial roles and their feature permissions are defined in UC-009. Authentication, account fields, access-reset mechanics, and initial Administrator provisioning remain open.
- Data classification, consent/legal basis, and remaining access/handling controls remain open. Retention and deletion rules for data other than attendance/evolution records remain open; those records are retained permanently and cannot be deleted. See [04 — Data and Integrations](./04-data-and-integrations.md).
